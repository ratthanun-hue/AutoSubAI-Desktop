1. FFmpeg (Bundled Media Engine Sidecar)
----------------------------------------
AutoSubAI Desktop invokes FFmpeg as an external standalone command-line
process (sidecar binary) for local audio extraction, media duration/resolution
probing, delogo blur bar, channel watermark overlay, and WYSIWYG subtitle
rendering.
